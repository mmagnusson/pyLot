Security advisories

pyLot matches the vendor, model, order number and firmware that devices report in a capture against security advisories in CSAF format, the format CISA publishes its ICS advisories in. Vendors such as Siemens, Schneider Electric and Hitachi Energy publish CSAF advisories of their own, which can be imported too. Each session's Vulnerabilities tab lists what matched.
