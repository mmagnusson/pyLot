How it works

Each upload becomes a session. pyLot maps the hosts and conversations in the capture and classifies devices with GRASSMARLIN's fingerprints: Modbus, DNP3, BACnet, EtherNet/IP, S7, IEC 104, PROFINET and over a hundred more industrial and IT protocols and vendors.
